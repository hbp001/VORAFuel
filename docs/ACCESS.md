# Data and weights: request access

The dataset originates from KRISO-provided vessel-operation and fuel measurements.
The research team performed quality control, time alignment, environmental-data
integration, model development and evaluation. Provider ownership is retained.

Dataset and task-specific weights are not published in this repository. They are
intended for private archival on Hugging Face and controlled provision on request.
Private archival does not make a public download or automatically approve sharing.

Start a GitHub issue with your institution and intended research use. The authors
will arrange a private contact channel and confirm permitted scope before release.
Do not include confidential data, authentication tokens or personal contact details
you do not want public. Availability of individual data sources remains subject to
provider restrictions. Weather data are not authorized for foundation pretraining.

Access packages distinguish task weights, scalers, processed evaluation inputs,
source measurements, weather-derived inputs and provenance. They include checksums.
No upstream foundation-model weights are redistributed. The private archive is not
an assurance of unconditional rights to redistribute each underlying source.
